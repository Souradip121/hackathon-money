## First, the honest maths

Prize money goes to the **team**. You split it.

| Prize | Team | Each person |
|---|---|---|
| ₹1 lakh | 4 people | ₹25,000 |
| ₹1 lakh | 6 (SIH 2024) | ≈ ₹16,700 |
| ₹1.5 lakh | 6 (SIH 2026) | ₹25,000 |
| ₹1.5 lakh | Solo | ₹1.5 lakh |

And only one team wins each prize.

**Can hackathons pay your fees?** A win can help a lot. But don't build your fee plan on it. Count it as a bonus.

Often the bigger prize is the interview. That's on the [PPI page](ppi.md).
