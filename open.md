All six are online, open to people in India, and need you to be 18+. Deadlines are in **IST**, converted from each page's own time zone, so you don't miss one by a night.

## 1. #BuildHybridAI (Google Firebase AI Logic)

- **Build:** an AI app for Web (Chrome), Android or iOS that runs prompts on-device when it can and falls back to the cloud, all through Firebase AI Logic. A significantly updated existing app counts too.
- **Prizes:** $30,000 in cash: a $10,000 grand prize, $7,000 and $4,000 for the runners-up, and $3,000 each for the best iOS, Android and Web/Chrome app. The grand prize's "interview" is a feature write-up, not a job interview.
- **Who:** open to India (it excludes Brazil, Italy, Quebec and sanctioned countries). Age of majority.
- **Closes:** 30 Nov, 2:00 PM PT = **1 Dec, 3:30 AM IST**. That's the first one to close, so start this weekend.
- **Rules:** [firebasehybridaihackathon.devpost.com/rules](https://firebasehybridaihackathon.devpost.com/rules)

## 2. Kiroween (AWS)

- **Build:** a new app made with Kiro, AWS's AI coding tool, in one of 4 Halloween categories (Resurrection, Frankenstein, Skeleton Crew, Costume Contest). Projects started before 30 Oct don't count.
- **Prizes:** $100K in total: $30,000 / $20,000 / $10,000, four $5,000 category prizes, $10,000 for Best Startup, and **4 Best Student Project prizes of $1,500 + a year of Kiro Pro Max**.
- **Who:** 18+, teams of up to 3. Devpost tags it beginner friendly.
- **Closes:** 4 Dec, 2:00 PM PT = **5 Dec, 3:30 AM IST**. The rules say no extensions.
- **Rules:** [kiroween.devpost.com/rules](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)

## 3. Reddit's Game of the Day Hackathon

- **Build:** a bite-sized daily game (puzzles, challenges, maps) on Devvit Web, Reddit's developer platform. React, Phaser, three.js, Godot or Unity all work.
- **Prizes:** $15,000 for the Best Daily Game, $6,000 for the Best Ported Daily Game, 10 honourable mentions of $1,000, 6 helper prizes of $500 and 5 feedback awards of $200.
- **Who:** 18+, teams allowed. Devpost tags it beginner friendly.
- **Closes:** 8 Dec, 6:00 PM PT = **9 Dec, 7:30 AM IST**.
- **Rules:** [redditgameofday2026.devpost.com/rules](https://redditgameofday2026.devpost.com/rules)

## 4. Codegeist 2026 (Atlassian)

- **Build:** an app on Atlassian's Forge platform that plugs into Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket or Rovo, for software teams, business teams or a specialised industry.
- **Prizes:** $120K in total. In each of 3 categories: $15,000 / $8,000 / $5,000. Plus 6 bonus prizes of $1,500 (Rovo agents, Atlassian MCP, Teamwork Graph, Marketplace apps) and swag for the first 300 eligible entries.
- **Who:** 18+, solo or team. Not student-only.
- **Closes:** 22 Dec, 12:00 PM PT = **23 Dec, 1:30 AM IST**. Winners around 15 Feb.
- **Rules:** [codegeist2026.devpost.com/rules](https://codegeist2026.devpost.com/rules)

## 5. Open Agentic Web Challenge (Cognizant)

- **Build:** an open-source "agentic website": agents that run in the browser and can find and work with other agents. The demo video must be under 2 minutes, in your own voice.
- **Prizes:** $5,000 + $1,500 in credits / $3,000 + $750 / $2,000 + $500.
- **Who:** 18+, teams allowed. When I checked, only about 50 people had registered, so your odds are good.
- **Closes:** 11 Jan, 8:00 AM PT = **11 Jan, 9:30 PM IST**.
- **Rules:** [cognizant-agenticweb.devpost.com/rules](https://cognizant-agenticweb.devpost.com/rules)

## 6. Name That LLM (Kaggle)

- **Build:** a model that works out which of 18 AI models wrote a paragraph. Submissions are Kaggle notebooks.
- **Prizes:** **$5,000 each for places 1 to 10** ($50K in total).
- **Who:** 18+, teams of up to 5.
- **Closes:** join or merge teams by **6 Jan**; final submission **13 Jan** (Kaggle deadlines are 11:59 PM UTC, which is 5:29 AM IST the next morning).
- **Page:** [kaggle.com/competitions/name-that-llm](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/name-that-llm)

## How to pick one this weekend

1. **Pick by deadline first.** If you have a free weekend now, #BuildHybridAI or Kiroween. If you're busy until exams end, Codegeist or Name That LLM.
2. **Read the judging criteria before you build.** Most of these score the idea, the use of the sponsor's tech and the demo video, not the amount of code.
3. **Submit a day early.** Devpost uploads slow down in the last hour, and none of these will extend for you.
