# AI tools for every part of college 🎓

Every tool from the reel, with links. Save this before exams.

## Doubts

- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/): turn on **Study Mode** (tools menu) so it teaches you step by step instead of handing over the answer.
- [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/): photograph the question and ask it to explain the method.
- [Claude](https://claude.ai/): good at long explanations and checking your reasoning. Paste your attempt and ask where it goes wrong.
- [Meta AI](https://www.meta.ai/): already inside WhatsApp. Open a chat with Meta AI for a quick doubt without leaving the app.

## Assignments

- [Gemini Deep Research](https://gemini.google.com/): pick Deep Research and it reads dozens of sources and writes a report with links. Use it to understand the topic, then write your own.
- [Consensus](https://consensus.app/): searches published research papers and shows what they found. Good for the "literature review" part.
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/): find papers and their citations. "Cite" under a result gives you the reference in IEEE, APA and more.
- [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/): free reference manager. Save papers as you read and it builds your bibliography in Word or Google Docs.

## Coding

- [Claude](https://claude.ai/): explain code, review your logic, help you debug.
- [Cursor](https://cursor.com/): an AI code editor that understands your whole project.
- [VS Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/): the free editor most of the industry uses.
- [GitHub](https://github.com/): put every project here. With your college ID, apply for the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) (free tools for students).

## PPTs and posters

- [Gamma](https://gamma.app/): paste your outline, get a designed deck in a minute. Then fix the content yourself.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/): templates for posters, certificates and slides.
- [Figma](https://www.figma.com/): for proper design work. [Figma for Education](https://www.figma.com/education/) is free for verified students.
- [Nano Banana](https://gemini.google.com/): Gemini's image model. Describe the poster or edit a photo with a sentence.

## Exam night: one NotebookLM notebook (now Gemini Notebook)

1. Open [Gemini Notebook](https://notebook.google.com/) (Google renamed NotebookLM) and make one notebook for the subject.
2. Add everything: your PDFs, the PYQs, slides, WhatsApp notes (as a PDF or pasted text), and YouTube lecture links.
3. Ask for a study guide of the most-asked topics in the PYQs, then a quiz, then flashcards.
4. Every answer cites your own sources, so you can check it against the notes.
