# College in 2025 vs 2026 🤯

The five swaps from the reel, what each new tool is actually for, and how to start today.

## Coursera → ChatGPT Study Mode

- **What it's for:** learning a topic instead of getting the answer. Study Mode asks you questions, gives hints and checks your understanding step by step.
- **How:** open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/), tap the tools menu in the message box and pick Study Mode. Then say what you're studying and your level.
- **Try:** "Teach me normalization in DBMS for my semester exam. Quiz me after each step."

## YouTube at 2x → NotebookLM (now Gemini Notebook)

- **What it's for:** turning your own notes, PDFs and lecture links into something you can study from. It answers from **your** sources only, with citations.
- **How:** open [Gemini Notebook](https://notebook.google.com/) (Google's new name for NotebookLM), make one notebook per subject and add your PDFs, slides and YouTube lecture links.
- **Try:** flashcards, a quiz, or an Audio Overview (a podcast of your notes) for the night before the exam.

## Google → Gemini

- **What it's for:** asking full questions instead of searching keywords, and Deep Research when you need a proper report with sources.
- **How:** [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com/). Upload a photo of the question or your notes if that's faster than typing.
- **Try:** "Explain the difference between TCP and UDP with an example from WhatsApp."

## Stack Overflow → Cursor

- **What it's for:** coding with an AI that has read your whole project, so it can explain the error and fix it where it happens.
- **How:** download [Cursor](https://cursor.com/). It looks like VS Code and opens your existing projects. There's a free plan with limited AI usage.
- **Try:** select the error, then ask "why is this happening, and what's the smallest fix?" Read the fix before you accept it.

## Photoshop → Nano Banana

- **What it's for:** making and editing images by describing them: fest posters, club banners, thumbnails.
- **How:** in the [Gemini app](https://gemini.google.com/), ask it to create or edit an image (Nano Banana is Gemini's image model). Upload a photo to edit it.
- **Try:** "Make a poster for our coding club's hackathon on 5 December, dark blue, neon green text, room for a QR code." Check every word it writes on the poster.

---

**One rule:** use these to learn faster, not to skip learning. Your college's rules on AI for assignments still apply.
