# How to match the judging criteria 🎯

Hackathons are rarely won by the "best" idea. They're won by the idea the organisers and sponsors **asked for**. Every track and every sponsor prize was written by someone who wants that problem solved, and sponsor prizes are often judged by the sponsor's own team. A unique idea nobody asked for is a risk.

So read your hackathon's track like a client brief.

## Step 1: Pull the brief apart (before day one)

For each track or prize you're aiming at, fill this in:

| Question | Where to find it | Your answer |
|---|---|---|
| What problem do they want solved? | Track description, problem statement | |
| Whose problem is it? (one real user) | Track description, sponsor's product | |
| What tech do they want used? | Sponsor prize rules ("best use of…") | |
| How is it scored? | Judging criteria on the event page | |
| Who judges it? | Event page, sponsor booth, opening ceremony | |

If the judging criteria aren't public, ask an organiser on day one. It's a normal question.

## Step 2: Ask the people who wrote it (day one)

Sponsors and mentors walk around on day one for a reason. Ask them, more than once if you need to:

1. "What would you actually use from this track?"
2. "What did last year's winners do that you liked?"
3. "What should we **not** build?"

Write down their exact words. You'll need them in step 4.

## Step 3: Build exactly that

- Keep the project inside the track. A side feature that's off-brief doesn't score.
- If there's a sponsor prize, make the sponsor's tool the **core** of the app, not a sticker. If you can delete their tool and your app still works, it's a sticker.
- Build one flow end to end, the one that solves the problem in the brief.

## Step 4: Use their words in your pitch

Map every judging criterion to one sentence and one moment in your demo:

| Criterion (their exact word) | Your sentence | Demo moment |
|---|---|---|
| e.g. Impact | "Meena saves 3 hours a week." | Before/after screen |
| e.g. Use of [sponsor tool] | "[Tool] is what makes the matching work." | The step that calls it |
| e.g. Technical depth | "We built X, not just a wrapper." | Architecture slide (10 s) |
| e.g. Presentation | Practise until it fits in 3 minutes | — |

When you say the judges' own words, you're filling in their score sheet for them.

**Be less original. Be more wanted.**
