# LocalSend: send your demo video at full quality 📲

Never send your hackathon demo video to yourself over WhatsApp. It gets compressed, and hackathon Wi-Fi is slow. Use **LocalSend** instead: a free, open-source app on GitHub that sends files between your own devices over the local network.

## How to use it

1. Install LocalSend on your phone **and** your laptop.
2. Connect both to the **same Wi-Fi**, or turn on your phone's hotspot and connect the laptop to it.
3. Open LocalSend on both. The other device shows up under **Send**.
4. Pick the video (or screenshots, the deck, the APK), tap the device, done. Full quality.

No internet needed: the files move over your local network, not through a server.

## If the devices don't see each other

- Venue or college **guest Wi-Fi often blocks devices from seeing each other** (client/AP isolation). Use your phone's hotspot instead.
- Allow LocalSend through the laptop's firewall when it asks.
- Make sure both devices are on the same network, not one on Wi-Fi and one on mobile data.

## Hackathon uses

- Phone-recorded demo video → laptop, for the submission.
- Teammates' screenshots → whoever makes the deck.
- The final build → every teammate's laptop, as a backup.

## Build on it

The repo is open for anyone. Read the code, contribute, or fork it as a starting point for your own hackathon project (keep the licence).
