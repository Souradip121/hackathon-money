# Hackathons where the prize is an interview 🎯

Cash is nice. But a pre-placement interview (PPI) at Amazon or Adobe can be worth far more than any prize.

One honest line first: **a PPI is an interview, not an offer.** You still have to clear it, so prep matters as much as the hackathon.

I checked all of these on 20 Oct 2026. Most 2026 rounds are over, so use this to plan for the next cycle.

## 1. Amazon ML Challenge

- **Usually:** September. In 2026, registration ran 7–22 Sep and the 72-hour challenge ran 25–27 Sep.
- **Who can enter:** full-time BE/B.Tech, ME/M.Tech, MS or PhD students at Indian engineering colleges graduating in **2027 or 2028**. Teams of 2–4, and cross-college teams are allowed.
- **Leads to:** **PPIs for the top 50 teams** for the Applied Scientist Intern role. The winning team also gets ₹1,00,000.
- **Official:** [Amazon ML Challenge 2026 on Unstop](https://unstop.com/hackathons/crp-amazon-ml-challenge-2026-amazon-1743604)
- **Prep tip:** Practise a full pipeline (clean the data, build a baseline, write the submission file) in under 6 hours. Then spend the other 66 improving it.

## 2. American Express CodeStreet

- **Usually:** July–August. In 2026, registration and ideas ran 13–27 Jul, prototypes 7–24 Aug, and the finale was 25 Aug.
- **Who can enter:** listings say final-year engineering students from participating colleges. Teams of 1–3, and everyone registers individually.
- **Leads to:** listings say **PPIs for the top 2 teams**. The official rewards tab didn't load for me, so check it.
- **Official:** [CodeStreet 2026 on HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/community/challenges/hackathon/codestreet-2026/)
- **Prep tip:** The idea round filters most teams. Fit the problem, the data and the prototype plan on one clear slide.

## 3. Flipkart GRiD

- **Usually:** June–July. GRiD 8.0 registration ran 19 Jun – 12 Jul 2026. Free and remote.
- **Who can enter:** B.Tech/BE, M.Tech/ME, MS, PhD or dual degree students graduating **2027–2030** (PhD up to 2031). You register alone, and teams of four form at the finals.
- **How it works:** profile screening, a 90-minute coding test, a case-study code round, then the top 48 (16 per track) go to Flipkart HQ in Bengaluru.
- **Leads to:** interviews for full-time roles and winter internships (4th year), or summer internships (3rd year).
- **Official:** [Flipkart GRiD 8.0](https://mycareernet.co/events/flipkart-earlycareers-grid-8-0)
- **Prep tip:** Round 1 screens your profile, so your GitHub and projects count before you write any code. Fix them now.

## 4. BNY Code Divas (women only)

- **Usually:** February–March. The 2026 cycle ran 27 Feb – 31 Mar.
- **Who can enter:** listings for 2026 asked for women in final year (2026 batch) of B.E./B.Tech in CS, IT or circuit branches, with 7+ CGPA and no backlogs. Solo.
- **Leads to:** in BNY's own words, top performers "stand a chance to secure a spot in our interview process". So it's a shot at interviews, not a guaranteed one. The 2026 role (Early Talent Analyst Trainee) was listed at ₹22 LPA total CTC.
- **Official:** [Code Divas on Mercer Mettl](https://xathon.mettl.com/event/BNYCodeDivas)
- **Prep tip:** Practise timed DSA rounds on a proctored platform, so the setup doesn't throw you on test day.

## 5. Tata Steel AI Hackathon

- **Usually:** May. The 2026 edition ran 22–31 May on HackerEarth.
- **Who can enter:** listings say final-year students (2026 batch), working professionals and freelancers. Solo.
- **Leads to:** listings say a **₹1 lakh joining bonus for the top 3** and up to 20 full-time offers.
- **Official:** [Tata Steel AI Hackathon on HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/community/challenges/competitive/tata-steel-ai-hackathon/)
- **Prep tip:** It's scored on a leaderboard. Submit a simple model on day 1, then improve it. Listings say outside datasets aren't allowed.

## 6. Adobe University Hackathon

- **Usually:** July–August. In 2026, registration closed 8 Aug and the finale ran 10–15 Oct.
- **Who can enter:** engineering UG and PG students, any year. Teams of 2–3, all from the same college. **Only the 2028 batch** can get the internship PPIs.
- **Leads to:** the **top 50 participants** "stand a chance" at a PPI and an internship paying ₹1,10,000 a month (Adobe's wording, so it isn't guaranteed). Each member of the winning team gets a MacBook Pro.
- **Official:** [Adobe University Hackathon 2026 on Unstop](https://unstop.com/hackathons/crp-adobe-university-hackathon-2026-adobe-1715333)
- **Prep tip:** Round 1 is 15 MCQs and 2 coding questions in one hour. Speed matters, so practise against a timer.

## 7. TCS CodeVita (Season 14: open now)

- **Usually:** registration in autumn, rounds Oct–Dec. Listings say Season 14 registration closes **13 Nov 2026**. MockVita 1 is 23–24 Oct, MockVita 2 is 13–14 Nov, and the pre-qualifier is 11–12 Dec.
- **Who can enter:** science or engineering UG/PG students finishing in **2027–2030**, from anywhere in the world. Solo, free. C, C++, Java and Python only.
- **Leads to:** **USD 20,000 shared by the top 3.** Past seasons have led to TCS interview calls. The official page I checked didn't list roles or salary bands for Season 14, so check there.
- **Official:** [codevita.tcsapps.com](https://codevita.tcsapps.com/)
- **Prep tip:** Do both MockVitas. They use the same 3-hour format as the real rounds.

## 8. HackOn with Amazon

- **Usually:** registration around May. Season 6.0 closed 28 May 2026.
- **Who can enter:** B.Tech, M.Tech, integrated dual degree or MCA students graduating in **2027 or 2028**, with 6.5+ CGPA. Teams of 2–3.
- **How it works:** a 100-minute proctored coding round, then a 48-hour build, then the finale.
- **Leads to:** **PPIs for top performers.** The winning team gets ₹1,00,000 (about ₹33,300 each for a team of 3).
- **Official:** [HackOn with Amazon 6.0 on Unstop](https://unstop.com/hackathons/crp-hackon-with-amazon-60-amazon-1682652)
- **Prep tip:** The coding round comes before any building. The whole team should practise DSA, not just your "coder".

## My prep plan for this winter ❄️

**Now – 13 Nov**

- Register for CodeVita. Do MockVita 1 (23–24 Oct) and MockVita 2 (13–14 Nov).
- Solve 2 DSA problems a day against a timer. Rotate arrays, strings, graphs and DP.

**Mid-Nov – Dec**

- CodeVita pre-qualifier: 11–12 Dec.
- Clean up your GitHub: 2 solid projects, each with a README, a demo GIF and a live link. GRiD and Adobe both screen profiles.

**Jan – Mar**

- Women: watch Flipkart Girls Wanna Code (January) and BNY Code Divas (Feb–Mar).
- Ask your placement office about HackWithInfy. In 2025, registration opened in February through colleges.
- Do one mock interview a week. Interview-prep sites like [Thita](https://thita.ai/) have DSA patterns and mock rounds.

**Apr – Sep**

- HackOn (May): 100-minute coding round, so keep the DSA habit going.
- Tata Steel AI (May) and Amazon ML (Sep): do one Kaggle-style problem a month so ML pipelines feel routine.
