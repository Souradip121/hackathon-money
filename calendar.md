# The 2027 hackathon calendar 🗓️

When every big hackathon for Indian students usually opens, with last year's exact dates and the official link.

**Read this first:** almost no 2027 dates are out yet. These months follow the 2026 cycle, so set your reminder **2 weeks before** each month and check the official page. Most stay open for just 2–4 weeks.

## How to use this

1. **Set a reminder 2 weeks before each month.** Most registrations close in 2–4 weeks.
2. **Many start with a coding test,** so keep up DSA all year, not just the week before.
3. **Check eligibility first:** batch year, CGPA cut-offs and "listed colleges only" rule out more people than the test does.
