# 11 hackathon sites: the 6 nobody tells you about, plus the 5 classics 🌍

Bookmark all eleven and check them every Sunday. Most hackathons fill up in the first week of registrations.

## The 6 beyond Devfolio and Unstop

| Site | Best for | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| [lablab.ai](https://lablab.ai/) | AI builders | Free online AI hackathons run by AI companies. Join solo or in a team, with Discord and mentors. Running now (Nov 2026): AMD AI League (to 1 Dec) and Vultr Agent Rush (3–8 Nov). |
| [ETHGlobal](https://ethglobal.com/events) | Web3 and crypto | Ethereum hackathons, online and in person. ETHGlobal Mumbai: 5–7 Nov 2026. |
| [DoraHacks](https://dorahacks.io/hackathon) | Keeping your project funded | Web3 and frontier-tech hackathons, plus grants for what you build after. |
| [Superteam Earn](https://superteam.fun/earn/) | Getting paid per task | Paid crypto (Solana) bounties and freelance gigs, all from one profile. (The old earn.superteam.fun link redirects here.) |
| [Kaggle Competitions](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions) | ML students | ML and data-science competitions with prize money, plus beginner Playground contests. |
| [TAIKAI](https://taikai.network/en/hackathons) | Global, online events | A hackathon platform used by companies, universities and foundations, many of them in Europe. Some are online. |

| Site | Best for |
|---|---|
| [Devpost](https://devpost.com/hackathons) | The biggest global list, online and in person |
| [Devfolio](https://devfolio.co/hackathons) | India's college and web3 hackathons |
| [MLH](https://www.mlh.com/seasons/2027/events) | The official student hackathon league, many beginner-friendly |
| [Unstop](https://unstop.com/hackathons) | Indian company and college competitions |
| [HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/challenges/hackathon/) | Company hackathons and hiring challenges |

## How to pick one this week

1. **Online first.** Your first hackathon shouldn't need a train ticket.
2. **Read the prizes and tracks before the theme.** Sponsor tracks are often easier to win than the main prize.
3. **Check eligibility** (students only? age? country?) before you build anything.
4. **Register the day it opens.** Team-matching channels are busiest in the first 48 hours.

Hack Club is great but only for ages 13–18, and Gitcoin no longer runs hackathons (it's a grants organisation now), so they're not on this list.
