# Hackathon team role checklist ✅

Don't build your hackathon team with your best friends. Not because they're bad, because they're exactly like you. Four coders can build the app, but judges also score the **demo**, the **pitch** and the **design**, and a team of four coders usually struggles with two of those.

Cover three jobs.

| Role | What they own | Ask them before you team up |
|---|---|---|
| 🛠 **Builder** | The core features: the one flow the demo shows has to work | "Show me something you built and shipped in a weekend." |
| 🎨 **Designer** | Makes the demo look clean: screens, colours, the slides | "Can you turn a rough screen into something a judge wants to look at?" |
| 🎤 **Presenter** | Explains the whole project in under 3 minutes and answers judge questions | "Explain your last project to me in 60 seconds." |

A team of 3–4 can cover these: two builders, one designer who also builds, and one presenter who also tests and writes the README.

## The most important one: pick people who show up

Skill matters less than this. Before the hackathon, check:

- [ ] They reply within a few hours when you message the group.
- [ ] They came to the planning call (or rescheduled instead of vanishing).
- [ ] They've read the problem statements or tracks before day one.
- [ ] They can stay for the whole event, including judging.
- [ ] They've said out loud which role they're taking.

## Split it before day one

- [ ] One builder owns the backend and data, one owns the frontend.
- [ ] The designer owns the look of the demo and the slide deck.
- [ ] The presenter owns the 3-minute pitch, the demo script and the backup demo video.
- [ ] Everyone has accounts and API keys ready (see the [5 tools](hack.md) page).
- [ ] You've agreed who sleeps when (two people awake at a time, never all four at 4 AM).

## And yes, you can still bring one friend

One friend is fine. Just make sure the other seats are filled by the roles above, not by a second and third copy of yourself.
