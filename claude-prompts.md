# Win your next hackathon with Claude, in 3 steps 🕷️

The prompts from the reel. Use them in order.

## Step 1: Research your problem statement

Open [Claude](https://claude.ai), turn on **Research** (you'll find it under the tools button in the chat box), and paste:

```
I'm at a hackathon. Here is the problem statement: [paste it]

Research it properly before we build anything:
1. Who has this problem, how often, and what does it cost them? Use real sources and cite them.
2. What already exists (apps, government portals, startups)? Why do people still have the problem?
3. What do the judges probably care about for this problem statement?
4. Give me 3 solution angles that existing products miss, and pick the one we can demo in 24-36 hours.
```

## Step 2: Know your tech stack

In the same chat, paste:

```
For the solution you picked, research the tech stack for a 24-36 hour build:
- The simplest frontend, backend and database that a team of [number] can ship, given we know [languages/frameworks].
- Free APIs, datasets or models we can use, with their limits.
- The single riskiest part to build, and a fallback if it doesn't work by hour 18.
- A build plan split by hour and by teammate.
```

## Step 3: Make the presentation

**Claude Design** (by Anthropic Labs) makes the deck from a conversation and exports to PowerPoint, PDF or Canva. It needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher).

```
Make a 7-slide hackathon pitch deck from everything in this chat, for a 3-minute pitch:
1 Hook, 2 Problem with one real number, 3 Solution, 4 Demo flow, 5 How it works, 6 Impact, 7 Team.
Big headlines, one idea per slide, no paragraphs.
```

**No paid plan?** Ask free Claude for that same slide-by-slide outline, then paste it into [Gamma](https://gamma.app) or Canva to design it.
