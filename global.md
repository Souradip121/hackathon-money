# Dollar-prize competitions I'm tracking 🌍

These pay in dollars (or crypto), and Indian students can enter. I checked each one on 20 Oct 2026.

Same rule as always: the prize goes to the **team**. $100,000 ÷ 4 = $25,000 each.

## Microsoft Imagine Cup 2027

- **Prizes:** **$100,000** to the Scale-track World Champion, plus a private mentorship session with Satya Nadella. **$50,000** for the Launch-track winner.
- **Which track:** Scale is for teams that already have users, revenue or funding (up to $5M). Launch is for early teams with no equity funding. Most student teams start in Launch.
- **Credits:** up to $5,000 in Azure credits after you register, to build your MVP.
- **Who:** enrolled students aged 18+. Teams of up to 4, and every member registers.
- **Deadline:** listed as **8 Jan 2027** for registration and submission. The official site still said "Imagine Cup 2027 – Coming Soon" when I checked, so confirm there.
- **Official:** [imaginecup.microsoft.com](https://imaginecup.microsoft.com/)

## Google Solution Challenge

- **2026 India edition** (GDG on Campus with Hack2skill): **₹10 lakh** in total cash prizes. Teams of 1–4, students 18+ enrolled in Indian colleges. It ran March to early July 2026.
- **The famous "$12,000":** in the 2023 global edition, each of the top 3 teams could win up to $12,000 ($3,000 per member). I couldn't find a 2026 dollar prize on an official Google page.
- **Official:** [Google Solution Challenge](https://developers.google.com/community/gdsc-solution-challenge) · [2026 India page](https://promptwars.in/solutionchallenge2026.html)
- **How to start:** join your college's GDG on Campus chapter. They run the kickoff sessions.

## Superteam Earn (honest odds)

- **What it is:** paid bounties and projects from crypto companies. Content (threads, videos), design, and dev work. Each listing shows its reward.
- **The honest part:** one bounty can get many submissions, and usually only the top few get paid. **Most entrants win nothing.** Treat it as practice and portfolio, and count any win as a bonus.
- **Getting paid:** rewards are in crypto, and winners may have to complete KYC. Read the Earn FAQ before you start.
- **Official:** [Superteam Earn](https://superteam.fun/earn/)

## Devpost online hackathons

- **What it is:** lots of online hackathons from global companies, many open to Indian students.
- **How to filter:** pick Online + Open. Then check whether the prize is cash or credits, which countries are eligible, and whether a demo video is required.
- **Official:** [devpost.com/hackathons](https://devpost.com/hackathons)

## How sponsor bounties actually pay

- **Each sponsor sets its own prizes.** At ETHIndia 2023, 1inch's Fusion API track paid $4,000 / $2,500 / $1,500 for 1st / 2nd / 3rd.
- **One project, several bounties.** You can usually enter one project for more than one sponsor prize. The limit changes by event, so read the rules.
- **Payment comes later.** Expect crypto or stablecoins, often after KYC. It can take weeks.
- **Tax:** prize money is income. Keep records and ask a CA.

## My 4-step "win a sponsor bounty" playbook

1. **Pick 2–3 sponsors before you arrive.** Read every prize page and choose ones whose requirements overlap.
2. **Make their tech the core.** Judges can spot a bolt-on in 10 seconds. If you remove their SDK, your app should break.
3. **Talk to the sponsor team early.** Show them a rough version on day 1 and ask what would impress them.
4. **Submit like the judge is tired.** Write a README with a "How we used X" section for each sponsor, add a 2-minute video and a deployed link, and tick the right tracks.
