# 5 hackathons only women can enter, and the prize is an interview 🎯

Every one of these is for women students in India, and each one leads to an interview or an internship at a big tech company.

One honest line first: **a PPI (pre-placement interview) is an interview, not an offer.** You still have to clear it.

All 2026 rounds are over, so this is your plan for the next cycle. I checked each one on its official page on 12 Nov 2026.

## The quick list

| Program | Usually opens | Who | What it leads to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Myntra WeForShe HackerRamp | June | 3rd/4th year B.Tech, teams of 2–3 | PPIs for final-year women in the top 6 teams |
| Meesho ScriptedByHer | June | Final year, any college | PPIs for every finalist team |
| Flipkart Girls Wanna Code | December | 2028 batch, solo | An SDE-1 internship interview for the cohort |
| Women Who Master (Logitech) | June | Any discipline, solo | Interviews for top performers |
| WiSE@TI (Texas Instruments) | April | 2nd year, 7.5+ CGPA | A chance to intern at TI India |

## 1. Myntra WeForShe HackerRamp

- **Leads to:** in Myntra's words, final-year participants in the **top 6 teams** get "Pre-Placement Interviews (PPIs) for internships at Myntra". The 2028 batch in the top 6 gets PPIs in the next cycle. Your PPI doesn't depend on your rank.
- **Who:** women in full-time B.Tech/B.E. (3rd or 4th year) or dual degree (4th or 5th year), graduating 2027 or 2028, 18+. **Teams of 2–3 from the same college.**
- **How it works:** online assessment (top 100 teams go through), then a prototype, then the Grand Finale at Myntra HQ, Bengaluru, with travel and stay arranged.
- **Last cycle:** registration 15 Jun – 2 Jul 2026, finale 12 Aug, PPIs on 13 Aug.
- **Prize:** rewards worth up to ₹5.25 lakh (cash + Myntra vouchers).
- **Official:** [WeForShe HackerRamp on mycareernet](https://mycareernet.co/mycareernet/contests/Myntra-WeForShe-Hacker-ramp-294)
- **Prep tip:** find your 2 teammates in your own college now. Cross-college teams aren't allowed.

## 2. Meesho ScriptedByHer

- **Leads to:** "Pre-Placement Interviews (PPIs) for all finalist teams", and a chance at a PPO (pre-placement offer).
- **Who:** final-year women engineering students from any college in India, any stream. Teams of 1–3 in 2026.
- **How it works:** the top 500 are shortlisted, then an idea round, a prototype round, and a finale at Meesho's Bengaluru office (trip paid).
- **Last cycle:** registration 8 – 19 Jun 2026. It started in 2025.
- **Prize:** ₹2.25 lakh in cash prizes.
- **Official:** [meesho.io/early-careers/scripted-by-her](https://www.meesho.io/early-careers/scripted-by-her) · [2026 edition on HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/challenges/hackathon/scriptedbyher-2026/)
- **Prep tip:** registration is open for just 12 days. Set a reminder for the first week of June.

## 3. Flipkart Girls Wanna Code

- **Leads to:** in Flipkart's words, "for the entire cohort, post one final assessment, we will be offering the interview process opportunity for the SDE-1 Internship". The chosen engineers also stand a chance at a PPI.
- **Who:** women in B.Tech, B.E., M.S. or a dual/integrated degree, **2028 batch** (in the 7.0 edition). Solo.
- **How it works:** screening on academics, a coding test (3 questions in 90 minutes), a learning cohort with mentors for the top 200, a day at Flipkart HQ, then the final assessment.
- **Last cycle (7.0):** 22 Dec 2025 – 20 Jan 2026, with 27,737 registrations. **8.0 isn't listed yet.** If it follows 7.0, it opens around late December, so it's the next one on this list.
- **Prize:** no cash. Merch, mentorship and a sponsored HQ visit for top performers.
- **Official:** [Girls Wanna Code on mycareernet](https://mycareernet.co/mycareernet/contests/Flipkart-Girls-Wanna-Code-266)
- **Prep tip:** do timed sets of 3 problems in 90 minutes from now until it opens.

## 4. Women Who Master (Aspire For Her × Logitech)

- **Leads to:** "Top performers will get placement and interview opportunities with Logitech."
- **Who:** women students of any discipline, plus recent graduates. **Solo.**
- **How it works:** an online MCQ round, a zonal round, then a Grand Finale at Logitech's Chennai office.
- **Last cycle:** registration 22 Jun – 21 Jul 2026, MCQ 25 Jul, zonal round 8 Aug, finale around 9–11 Sep.
- **Prize:** ₹1,00,000 / ₹75,000 / ₹50,000.
- **Official:** [Women Who Master on Unstop](https://unstop.com/hackathons/crp-women-who-master-aspire-for-her-x-logitech-1702474)
- **Prep tip:** it's open to every discipline, so it's the one to send to friends outside CS.

## 5. WiSE@TI Hackathon (Texas Instruments)

- **Leads to:** "Select students will have the chance to intern with Texas Instruments India." The 2026 official page lists mentorship and the internship chance, **no cash prize**.
- **Who:** women in CS, IT, electrical, electronics, communication or related fields, graduating 2028: 2nd-year bachelor's, 1st-year master's or 3rd-year dual degree. **CGPA 7.5+**, no active backlogs. Teams of 3–4, formed by TI.
- **Last cycle:** registration 6 – 29 Apr 2026, round 2 in late May, finale in Bengaluru in the second week of June.
- **Official:** [WiSE@TI on mycareernet](https://mycareernet.co/mycareernet/contests/Texas-Instruments-WiSE-TI-Hackathon-for-Wome-277)
- **Prep tip:** it's the one for 2nd-years. Keep your CGPA above 7.5 this semester.

## One more: BNY Code Divas

Women in final year (2026 batch: B.E./B.Tech in CS, IT or circuit branches, 7+ CGPA), solo. Top performers "stand a chance to secure a spot in our interview process", and the 2026 role was listed at ₹22 LPA. The 2026 cycle ran 19 Feb – 31 Mar. Prizes are gift vouchers. [Code Divas on Mercer Mettl](https://xathon.mettl.com/event/BNYCodeDivas)

## Not on this list, and why

- **Google Girl Hackathon:** the official pages now redirect, and there's been no edition since 2025.
- **Walmart CodeHers:** no edition since Jan–Mar 2025.
- **Amex Makeathon:** nothing since 2024.
